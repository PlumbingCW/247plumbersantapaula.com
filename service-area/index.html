Nearby towns we serve

FillmoreOjaiVentura

Fillmore is the nearest separate city, east on State Route 126. Ojai is in the Ojai Valley to the north by State Route 150. Ventura is the larger coastal city to the west.

What to know about each town

Fillmore, ZIP code 93015, is a small city in the Santa Clara River Valley with a historic downtown on Central Avenue. Older downtown buildings and newer homes on the edges need different repairs, so we ask the age of the property.

Ojai, ZIP code 93023, is in the Ojai Valley, and most of its homes were built between 1940 and 1980. That era often means galvanized steel, aging copper and older drains. Ask your water provider about pressure and water quality.

Ventura, ZIP codes 93001, 93003 and 93004, is the largest nearby city. Downtown has older commercial buildings, the east side includes Saticoy and Montalvo and the hillsides rebuilt after the 2017 Thomas Fire have a mix of new and old lines.

Plumbing problem in any of these towns? Our line is answered 24/7.

If you already know what is wrong, start with the service page. For a slow drain, see drain cleaning. For no hot water, see water heater repair. For a wet spot and a high bill, see leak detection. For water pouring from a pipe, see burst pipe repair.
