Emergency Faucet Repair in Santa Paula, CA

Faucet gushing, shutoff valve stuck or shower leaking? Emergency faucet and valve repair in Santa Paula, CA, 24/7.

Call (805) 249-7135

Faucet repair fixes dripping spouts, failed cartridges, leaking handles, broken shutoff valves and sink supply lines. In Santa Paula, CA we repair kitchen, bath and utility fixtures and replace the ones that are worn out.

A faucet is the end of a small plumbing system. The handle moves a cartridge, the cartridge opens a path from the supply lines, and the supply lines connect through shutoff valves to the house. A failure can be at any link.

The emergency version is a valve that will not turn off, a supply line that burst under the sink, or a shower valve leaking inside the wall.

When a faucet leak becomes an emergency

A dripping faucet is an annoyance. A supply line spraying water under a sink is an emergency. We answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and offer same-day appointments when available.

Locate the shutoff valve under the sink or at the wall. If it will not turn, shut off the main water valve to the house and call us.

Stop the water first

  • Turn the angle stop valve under the sink clockwise.
  • If it does not turn, close the main valve for the whole house.
  • Open a lower faucet to drain pressure and slow the leak.
  • Move items out of the cabinet and mop up water to protect the cabinet floor.

Kitchen and bathroom faucets

Dripping at the spout usually means a worn cartridge or seats. Leaks at the base or handle mean worn seals. Many faucets can be repaired, but cheaper models are often better replaced.

Shutoff valves that will not shut off

Old angle stops and gate valves corrode and fail. If the valve under your sink will not turn off, a faucet repair becomes a whole-house shutoff. We replace the stop first so future repairs are easy.

Shower and tub valves

A shower that will not shut off or a tub spout that drips often needs a new cartridge or a replacement valve. When the valve is leaking inside the wall, we find the access point and the leak before we open the wall. See leak detection for hidden leaks.

Kitchen sink fixtures

Sprayers, dishwasher connections and the garbage disposal all share the sink cabinet. If a leak is under the sink, we check each connection. A leaking disposal is covered on our garbage disposal repair page.

Replacement

When a faucet is more than a few years old and starting to fail in several places, replacement is the better value. We install your fixture or help you choose one. See plumbing installation.
