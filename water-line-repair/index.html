Emergency Water Line Repair in Santa Paula, CA

Soggy yard, sudden low pressure or no water at all? Emergency water line repair in Santa Paula, CA, 24/7.

Call (805) 249-7135

The water service line runs underground from the meter to your house. In Santa Paula, CA, when it leaks or breaks, the signs are a wet spot in the yard, a sudden drop in pressure, a higher bill or no water at all.

Because the line is buried, the leak can run for a long time before it reaches the surface. We locate the break, repair or replace the section and test the line before it is covered.

Ownership matters. Usually the utility owns the line up to and including the meter, and the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the house. Ask your water utility where your responsibility starts.

Water line break: act quickly

A broken service line can wash out soil, flood a yard and leave you without water. We answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and offer same-day appointments when available.

Shut the valve at the house if you can. If water is pouring out near the meter, call your water utility as well, since the meter side may be theirs.

Immediate steps

  • Shut the main valve at the house.
  • Do not dig near the leak, since other utilities may be close.
  • Keep children and pets away from soft, wet ground.
  • Take photos of the wet area and note when you first saw it.

Finding a buried leak

A wet patch does not always sit right above the break, because water follows the path of least resistance. We pressure test the line and use listening tools to mark the spot before we dig.

See leak detection for hidden leaks, and burst pipe repair for lines inside the house.

Digging and utilities

Before any digging, utilities must be marked. California's 811 service is how buried lines are located, so call before you dig and ask us how we schedule it. We then excavate, repair and backfill.

Replacing the line

Older lines made of galvanized steel or aging copper tend to fail in more than one place. Replacing the whole run gives you one new line instead of a series of patches. We describe the material options and what they mean for your yard.

Restoring the surface

We backfill the trench and leave the surface level. Landscaping, concrete and paving repairs are handled by other trades, and we tell you in advance what to expect.

Related plumbing

A line break can leave debris in the pipes. After the repair, run cold water at the lowest faucet for a few minutes. If your pressure is still low, see repiping.

Work on the water service line is governed by the California Plumbing Code and by your water utility's own rules at the meter. Ask both before anyone digs.
