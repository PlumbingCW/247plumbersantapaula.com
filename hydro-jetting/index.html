Emergency Hydro Jetting in Santa Paula, CA

Snaked it and it clogged again? Emergency hydro jetting in Santa Paula, CA clears roots, grease and scale from the pipe wall.

Call (805) 249-7135

Hydro jetting is a drain cleaning method that sends high pressure water through a hose and a rotating nozzle to scour the inside of a pipe. For Santa Paula, CA homes and businesses, it is the answer when a cable machine only punched a hole through a blockage and the line closed up again a few weeks later.

A cable cuts a channel through the middle. Water jetting washes the whole circumference, so grease coatings, scale and fine roots come off the wall instead of staying behind. That matters in older clay and cast iron lines, where rough interior surfaces catch every new bit of debris.

We start with a camera when the line is old or the history is unknown. Jetting a pipe that has a crack or a collapsed joint can wash soil in, so the picture decides whether jetting is safe.

When a stubborn clog becomes an emergency

Callers who need jetting have often already tried a cable. If the cable stuck, or the drain backed up again the same week, the main line may be mostly closed and sewage could start rising at the lowest drain. We answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and offer same-day appointments when available.

Tell the dispatcher what has been tried and how far a cable went. That helps us decide whether to bring the jetter first.

While you wait for a jetting visit

  • Stop using fixtures that drain to the blocked line, especially toilets and the washing machine.
  • Locate the exterior cleanout and clear the area around it so we can reach it.
  • Do not run a garden hose into the cleanout.
  • Move belongings away from the lowest drain, such as a basement or garage floor drain.

Roots, grease and scale in Santa Paula lines

Santa Paula's older neighborhoods have mature trees and old lateral lines. Roots enter through joints, find the moisture in the pipe and grow into a mat. A cable removes the mat but not the root hairs along the wall. Jetting does a better job, and our sewer line cleaning page explains how to schedule it as maintenance.

Restaurants and food prep businesses along Main Street fight grease. For commercial kitchens, jetting is often part of a routine schedule. See commercial plumbing for grease trap and line maintenance.

Camera first, jetter second

A short camera run before jetting tells us the pipe material, the condition and the location of any breaks. If the pipe is cracked, jetting it is a bad idea. A camera also gives you a recording to compare with a later run after the line is clean. Read our sewer inspection page for the details.

What a jetting visit looks like

  1. Access

    We find a cleanout or pull a fixture to reach the line.

  2. Camera

    A quick look at the pipe condition and the clog.

  3. Jetting

    We work the nozzle up and down the line in passes.

  4. Verify

    A second camera pass shows the pipe wall is clean.

When not to jet

Cracked clay, offset joints and thin cast iron can fail under pressure. If the camera shows damage, we recommend repair first. See sewer line repair for those options.

What affects hydro jetting cost

The price is clear before work starts. These are the factors:

A short 4 inch line costs less to jet than a long 6 inch main.

Light scale is quick. Heavy roots and grease need more passes.

A cleanout makes the job straightforward. Pulling a toilet to get in adds time.

Inspection adds time and gives you a record of the line.
