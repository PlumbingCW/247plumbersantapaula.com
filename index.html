Local Plumbing

Plumbing in Santa Paula, California

Santa Paula sits on the north bank of the Santa Clara River in Ventura County, with State Route 126 running through town. We serve ZIP codes 93060 and 93061. When a pipe fails at midnight, a local line means you reach people who know these streets.

The Main Street historic district downtown has some of the oldest bungalow, Victorian and craftsman homes in the city, near landmarks like the California Oil Museum and the Santa Paula Train Depot. Homes of that age can still have original galvanized steel or early copper pipe and older cast iron drains, so we check the age of the lines before we quote a repair.

Newer master-planned areas such as Harvest at Limoneira are newer builds, and East Santa Paula has a mix of ages. Tell us your neighborhood and the age of the house when you call, and we will plan the visit around the house you have.

Water that cannot wait is an emergency in any neighborhood. We answer 24/7, so a Sunday night leak gets the same answer as a Tuesday morning one.
