Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Santa Paula, CA

Pipe burst and water running? Emergency burst pipe repair in Santa Paula, CA, answered 24/7.

Call (805) 249-7135

Burst pipe repair stops the water from a failed supply pipe and then repairs or replaces the damaged section. In Santa Paula, CA, burst or cracked pipes come from old corroded copper, galvanized steel that has rusted through, failed fittings and, now and then, a hard impact during construction work.

The first minutes matter. Every minute water runs can soak drywall, flooring and insulation. Stop the water, call a plumber and then deal with the repair.

Older homes with galvanized pipe or aging copper have more failures. We can often repair a section and tell you whether the rest of the pipe is a risk.

Pipe burst: first five minutes

Water from a burst pipe does not stop on its own. Shut the main valve and call. We answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and offer same-day appointments when available.

Know where your main shutoff is before an emergency. It is usually at the front of the house near the hose bib or in the garage.

Right now

  • Close the main water valve, turning it clockwise until it stops.
  • Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and relieve pressure.
  • Switch off the power at the panel if water has reached outlets or appliances and the floor near the panel is dry.
  • Photograph the damage and move valuables out of the wet area.

What causes a pipe to burst

Common burst pipe causes.

Failed fitting or jointAnywhere pipes connectReplace the fitting.
High water pressureSeveral weak points at onceInstall a pressure regulator.
Impact or construction damageNear renovationsRepair the pipe.

Repairing the pipe

We cut out the damaged section and join new pipe with the right fittings. In accessible places, this is a short job. In walls and ceilings, we open a small access hole and close it after the repair.

If the pipe is old and corroded elsewhere, we tell you. A repair may last for years, or it may be the first of several failures.

Pressure and shutoff valves

Water pressure that is too high stresses pipes and fixtures. A regulator at the main brings it down. Ask us to check the pressure while we are on site.

Water damage afterward

We repair plumbing, not restoration. For wet drywall and flooring, you will need a water damage company, and your insurance carrier can guide you. We help document the source.

When repair is not enough

If the pipes throughout the house are corroded, repeated bursts are likely. See repiping for replacing the lines. If the leak is hidden, see leak detection, and if it is outside the house, see water line repair.
