Looking for an emergency plumber in Ventura, CA? Call (805) 249-7135. We are based in Santa Paula, about 13 miles east of Ventura, and we dispatch across the city at any hour. The line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Officially the City of San Buenaventura, Ventura is the largest city near Santa Paula, with 110,763 residents at the 2020 census. The Ventura River runs through its west side and the Santa Clara River forms its southern boundary. Neighborhoods differ a lot in age, so we plan by area.

Ventura is farther from our Santa Paula base than most of the towns we serve, so we tell you plainly what to expect when you call.

Downtown Ventura and the Main Street core

Downtown has buildings from the 1920s oil boom, such as the Ventura Theatre of 1928. Main Street has been closed to vehicles since June 2020, which matters to businesses because access for service vehicles can be tight. Tell us about loading access when you call.

Older buildings often have cast iron drains that corrode and trap grease. Restaurants and shops should ask about hydro jetting, sewer inspection and commercial plumbing.

Saticoy and Montalvo on the east side

Saticoy sits at the start of the Santa Clara River Valley, and the city annexed the last unincorporated parts of Montalvo in 2012. These east side areas are nearer to us along State Route 126 and the Metrolink area. Tell us your address and the age of the home and we plan the visit.

Hillside homes after the Thomas Fire

The 2017 Thomas Fire destroyed 504 homes in the city, mostly in hillside neighborhoods. Rebuilt houses have newer plumbing and their older neighbors do not, so pipe age can change from lot to lot. Homes at the bottom of a slope can see higher water pressure, so a regulator and a gauge reading are worth checking, see burst pipe repair.

When a main sewer line backs up in Ventura

A main line problem shows up at the lowest drain first, often a shower or tub on the ground floor, and then more than one fixture is affected. Gurgling toilets and water rising in a tub when you run the washing machine are common signs. Stop using water and call.

Cleaning may restore flow, but a camera tells you whether the cause is grease, roots or a broken pipe. Older downtown buildings and older east side homes are where we see this most often.
