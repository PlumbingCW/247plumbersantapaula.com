Emergency Toilet Repair in Santa Paula, CA

Overflowing, leaking at the base or running nonstop? Emergency toilet repair in Santa Paula, CA, 24/7.

Call (805) 249-7135

Toilet repair covers the clogs, leaks, running water and loose bases that make a toilet unusable. In Santa Paula, CA, we repair fill valves, flappers, wax rings and flanges, and we replace toilets that have cracked or reached the end of their life.

Most toilet problems start small. A flapper that does not seal wastes water silently. A wobble at the base slowly breaks the wax seal and leaks under the floor. A clog that you plunge once and ignore may be a sign of something deeper in the line.

Homes in older parts of town may have floors that have been wet for years without anyone noticing. We check the subfloor when we pull a toilet.

Overflowing toilet: stop it first

An overflowing toilet is an emergency because the water keeps coming until you stop it. Turn the supply valve behind the toilet clockwise to shut off the fill. We answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and offer same-day appointments when available.

If more than one fixture is backing up, the problem is not the toilet. It is the line behind it, and we treat it as a drain call.

While the toilet is out of order

  • Close the shutoff valve at the wall behind the toilet.
  • Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop water entering the bowl.
  • Use towels and a wet vacuum on spills and avoid skin contact.
  • Do not keep flushing, because it will not clear the clog.

Running toilet

A toilet that runs on and off has a leaking flapper or a worn fill valve. Dye in the tank, left a few minutes, shows color in the bowl if the flapper leaks. A small leak can add noticeably to a water bill, so fix it early.

Leaks at the base

Water at the base of the toilet usually comes from the wax ring that seals the bowl to the drain. A wobbling toilet breaks it. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange and the floor, replace the ring and set the toilet level. If the floor is soft, we tell you before we reset anything.

Clogs

A plunger or a toilet auger clears most bowl clogs. If it returns the same week, the cause may be a partial blockage deeper in the line. Our drain cleaning team can cable the branch and check the main.

Toilet installation and replacement

Older toilets can be inefficient and prone to leaks. We install replacements and check that the flange is the right height and in good shape. For new bathrooms or remodels, see plumbing installation.

Fixtures and valves nearby

Supply lines and shutoff valves under the toilet wear out as well. A valve that will not turn can make every other repair harder. See faucet and valve repair for how we replace them.
