Water Softener and Filtration in Santa Paula, CA

Spots on glassware, scale on fixtures or a taste you do not like? Water softener and filtration installation in Santa Paula, CA.

Call (805) 249-7135

Water softeners and filters change what comes out of your tap. In Santa Paula, CA, homeowners ask about scale on fixtures, spotty dishes, dry skin after showers and taste or odor. The right system depends on what is actually in your water.

We install and service softeners, whole-house sediment and carbon filters and point-of-use filters. We start with a question: what do you want to fix?

We do not claim to know your water quality from memory. Test your tap, or ask your water utility for its latest water quality report, and we plan from there.

Start with a water test

Hardness, chlorine, sediment and other measures vary by source and by season. A simple home test kit or a lab test tells you what is in your water. Your water utility publishes a consumer report that lists what it tests for.

A softener addresses hardness. A carbon filter addresses chlorine taste and odor. A sediment filter catches grit. Matching the system to the problem avoids paying for something you do not need.

Softener installation

A softener ties into the main water line, needs a drain for regeneration and an outlet for power. We choose a location that is accessible for salt refills and keep hose bibs on unsoftened water if you prefer. Salt-free systems are a different design, so ask which type matches your goal.

Whole-house and under-sink filters

Whole-house filters sit at the main line and treat water for every tap. Under-sink systems treat drinking and cooking water only. Filters need cartridge changes on a schedule, and we label the dates when we install.

Service and replacement

If your system is leaking, no longer regenerating or producing off-tasting water, we service it or replace it. Scale can also damage water heaters. See water heater repair if your heater makes popping noises.

Plumbing around the system

Installation includes shutoff valves, a bypass and connections to the drain. See plumbing installation for related fixture work.

Questions to ask before you buy a system

  • What result do I want, softer water, better taste or less sediment?
  • Has my water been tested, and what did the test show?
  • Where will the unit go, and is there a drain and an outlet nearby?
  • What does ongoing salt or cartridge replacement cost, and who does it?

Answering these first keeps you from buying equipment that does not match the problem. We are happy to talk through a test result by phone and tell you what it means for your plumbing.
