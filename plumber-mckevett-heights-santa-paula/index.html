McKevett Heights is a named neighborhood in Santa Paula. It appears in the local real estate tract list for the city, and Limoneira's company history ties it to the city's founding families and says it was laid out in the 1920s. It sits on the hillside above the city, and we use anchor streets such as Glen Way and Virginia Terrace to describe where we mean.

Homes here were built across different decades, so no two jobs look the same. Call (805) 249-7135, tell us the street and the year the house was built if you know it, and we plan around the house you actually have.

Where McKevett Heights is

No official map of McKevett Heights is published, so we do not claim exact boundaries. Real estate listings group homes on Glen Way and nearby streets under the McKevett Heights name. A county heritage survey of Santa Paula describes Spanish Colonial Revival and English Tudor houses from the 1920s and 1930s along Virginia Terrace and Glen Way. Limoneira's history says the hillside above the city developed on land given by the Hardison and Teague families.

If you are not sure whether your address counts as McKevett Heights, it does not matter for us. Give us the street address and we confirm that we serve it.

Homes of many ages, many remodels

Listing sites describe a mix of older and newer homes here. In a neighborhood like that, the plumbing in one house can look very different from the house next door. A kitchen may have been remodeled while the bathroom is original, or a room may have been added decades after the first build.

That mix is why the first questions we ask are practical. When was the house built, which rooms were remodeled and has anyone replaced the water heater or the main shutoff. The answers tell us where to start. We never assume what is behind a wall because of the year on a deed.

Planning a bathroom or kitchen remodel

Remodels are a common reason to call a plumber in an older neighborhood. Moving a sink, adding a shower or hooking up a new appliance all touch supply and drain lines. Our plumbing installation service covers new fixtures and appliance hookups, and we give you a clear price before work starts.

Before you start, ask the City of Santa Paula building department whether your project needs a permit. Requirements vary with the scope of work, so we do not quote rules from memory.

Water quality and your tap

Water taste, spots on glass and scale on fixtures vary from home to home. If you wonder about your water, test your tap first and ask your water utility what is in the supply. Then we can talk about whether a softener or filter makes sense for your situation. See water softener and filtration.
