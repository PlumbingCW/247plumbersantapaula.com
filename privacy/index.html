Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use this site or call our line.

Call (805) 249-7135

This policy explains how Santa Paula 24/7 Plumbing handles information when you use this website or call our line.

Information we collect

This website has no forms, so we do not collect information through it. If you call us, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so we can book and perform the work.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use the details you give us to schedule appointments, perform the work, follow up on it and keep records. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This site loads a web font from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address under their own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@247plumbersantapaula.com. Last updated November 14, 2026.
