A tankless water heater heats water as it flows, so there is no storage tank to run out. In Santa Paula, CA, we install tankless units, repair ones that throw error codes or run cold, and flush and descale units that have lost output.

Tankless units are efficient, but they are less forgiving than a tank. They need the correct gas line size, a proper vent and a periodic flush. Skip any of those and the unit shows it as cold water, error codes or a shortened life.

Many callers want service on a tankless unit installed by someone else. We check what is wrong first and tell you plainly whether we can repair it or whether it needs replacement.

Flushing and descaling a tankless unit

Mineral scale builds inside the heat exchanger over time. Scale narrows the water path, so the unit overheats, throws errors and delivers less hot water. A flush pushes a descaling solution through the heat exchanger and then rinses it out.

How often depends on your water. Ask your water utility for its annual water quality report, or test your tap, to see how mineral rich your supply is. Mineral rich supplies scale faster.

Installing a tankless unit

Switching from a tank to tankless usually means a larger gas line, a new vent through the wall or roof, and a condensate drain on high efficiency models. We check these before quoting so you are not surprised.

We also size the unit to your fixtures. A family running two showers and a dishwasher needs a different flow rate from a couple in a small bungalow.

Repair or replace

If the unit is within its service life and the part is available, repair is usually the right call. If it has failed repeatedly, see water heater replacement. For a first install, our water heater installation page covers planning.

Do not delay descaling

A unit that is flushed on schedule runs longer. One that is ignored can fail in ways that void a warranty, so check the manufacturer's maintenance requirements and keep a record.

What to confirm before switching to tankless

A tankless unit needs enough gas supply or electrical capacity to heat water on demand, plus proper venting. Many homes with a tank need the gas line upsized or a vent run changed. We check these before you buy the unit, so the price you are given matches the job.

Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Santa Paula building department what applies. Also ask us about the flow you need. A household that runs two showers and a dishwasher at once needs a different unit from one person living alone.

The California Plumbing Code and the maker's instructions both apply to venting and gas piping on a tankless unit. Ask the city building department how permits are handled for your address.
