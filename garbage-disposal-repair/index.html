Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Santa Paula, CA

Disposal jammed, humming or leaking under the sink? Emergency garbage disposal repair in Santa Paula, CA, 24/7.

Call (805) 249-7135

Garbage disposal repair fixes jams, leaks, electrical failures and clogs in the disposal and the drain it connects to. In Santa Paula, CA we repair, replace and install disposals in kitchens of every age.

A disposal that hums but does not spin is usually jammed. One that does nothing may have a tripped reset button or a failed switch. One that leaks has a failed seal, a loose mounting or a cracked housing.

Because the disposal sits at the start of the kitchen drain, a failure often looks like a clogged sink. We check the disposal before we cable the line.

Disposal leaking or sink backed up

A leaking disposal can flood a cabinet, and a jammed disposal can leave a sink full of dirty water. We answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and offer same-day appointments when available.

Switch the disposal off at the wall and never put your hand into it. Use tongs if you must remove something.

Disposal emergency steps

  • Turn the disposal off at the switch and unplug it under the sink if you can reach the cord.
  • Close the supply valve if water is leaking from the dishwasher connection.
  • Place a bowl under the leak and wipe the cabinet floor.
  • Do not run the dishwasher until the sink drains again.

Jams and humming

A humming disposal has a jammed flywheel. Many jams clear with the hex key slot under the unit and the reset button. If it jams again, the flywheel may be damaged. We assess whether repair makes sense.

Leaks

A leak at the flange needs plumber's putty and a new seal. A leak from the housing means the disposal has failed and should be replaced. Leaks from the dishwasher hose and the drain connection are simple to fix.

Replacing a disposal

We install new units in place of old ones, matching the mounting and the electrical connection. If you want to add a disposal, we also install a switch or air switch where needed. A new disposal also needs a connection to the dishwasher drain.

What goes down a disposal

Grease, fibrous peels, coffee grounds in bulk and bones cause jams and clogs. Run cold water while it is running and for a few seconds after. Those habits can extend its life.
