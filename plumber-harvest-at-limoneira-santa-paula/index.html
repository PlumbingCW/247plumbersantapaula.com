Harvest at Limoneira is a master-planned community on the east side of Santa Paula. It was built on the former Teague McKevett Ranch. Limoneira announced that the ranch land known as East Area 1 was annexed into the City of Santa Paula on February 12, 2013. Construction began in 2016.

These are newer homes, so the plumbing questions are different from those in an older neighborhood. Call (805) 249-7135 if a fixture, water heater or leak needs attention, and tell us your street and whether you own or rent.

About the community

Limoneira's announcements describe roughly 500 acres. The original agreement called for 1,500 homes. Phase 1, the Haun Creek Neighborhood, has 707 lots that were sold to builders including Lennar, KB Home, K. Hovnanian and Richmond American. In March 2024 the city council approved 550 more homes, made up of 250 single-family homes and 300 for-rent duplexes.

Lennar lists its homes at East Santa Paula and Harvest Loop and describes single-family detached homes with a pool, a clubhouse called The Farmhouse and sports fields. The community is still growing, so we do not give a count of finished homes. We rely on the builder and developer descriptions above.

Newer does not mean problem free

A newer home usually has newer fixtures and plumbing. It can still have a loose connection, a faulty valve or a heater that needs adjusting. Problems with new work often show up in the first years, once everything has been used every day.

Our approach is simple. We look at the symptom, check the likely cause and tell you what we found before any work begins. A small leak detection visit can settle whether a damp spot is a plumbing problem or condensation.

Warranty first, then call us

If your home is still under a builder warranty, read it before you hire anyone. Some repairs belong to the builder, and work by someone else can affect coverage. Call the builder about anything the warranty covers. Call us for repairs outside it, for upgrades or when you want a second opinion.

If you rent one of the duplexes or another rental home, tell your landlord or property manager first. Many leases require that repairs go through the owner. We can help once the owner approves the call.
