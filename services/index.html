Every job starts with a call to (805) 249-7135. We answer 24/7 and offer same-day appointments when available, with a clear price before work starts. Pick a service below to learn how we handle it.

Drains and sewer lines

Drain Cleaning

Clogged sinks, tubs, toilets and main lines, snaked or cleared with the right tool.

Hydro Jetting

High pressure water scouring for roots, grease and scale in drain and sewer lines.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Main sewer line clearing for roots, grease and backups.

Sewer Inspection

Camera inspections of drain and sewer lines for recurring clogs, home purchases and damage.

Sewer Line Repair

Spot repairs, trenchless pipe lining and replacement for cracked, rooted or collapsed sewer lines.

Water heaters

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, leaking tanks, pilot and thermocouple problems on gas, electric and hybrid units.

Water Heater Replacement

Failed tank swaps, same-size or upgrades to tankless and heat pump models.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs of tank, tankless and heat pump water heaters.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless install, repair, flushing and descaling.

Leaks and pipes

Leak Detection

Meter running, high water bill, wet walls and ceilings, slab and underground leaks located without tearing out finishes.

Slab Leak Repair

Finding and fixing leaking water lines under a concrete slab, with options to reroute.

Burst Pipe Repair

Burst, cracked and leaking supply pipes shut off, repaired and replaced.

Water Line Repair

Repair and replacement of the underground water service line from the meter to your home.

Repiping

Whole-house and partial repiping for homes with failing galvanized, corroded copper or leaking lines.

Fixtures, installs and water quality

Toilet Repair

Running, leaking, clogged and wobbling toilets repaired or replaced.

Faucet Repair

Dripping faucets, failed shutoff valves, shower valves and sink leaks.

Garbage Disposal Repair

Jammed, leaking and humming disposals repaired or replaced, including new installs.

Plumbing Installation

Fixture, appliance and line installation for remodels, additions and replacements.

Water Softener and Filtration

Softeners, whole-house filters and point-of-use systems installed and serviced.

Businesses and rentals

Commercial Plumbing

After-hours and scheduled plumbing for shops, restaurants, offices and rental properties.
