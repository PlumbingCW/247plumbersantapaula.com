About Santa Paula 24/7 Plumbing

A call-only plumbing line based in Santa Paula, answered 24/7 with a clear price before work starts.

Call (805) 249-7135

A local line for Santa Paula plumbing

Santa Paula 24/7 Plumbing is a call-only plumbing line for Santa Paula, California. You call (805) 249-7135, tell us what is happening and we arrange a plumbing appointment, same-day when available.

We keep the business simple. There are no online forms and no scheduling apps. A real person answers the line, and you hear the plan and a clear price before work starts.

What we do

We repair and replace the plumbing that homes and small businesses depend on. That includes drains, sewer lines, water heaters, hidden leaks and burst pipes, plus fixtures, installations and commercial plumbing. See all services.

How we work

Our standards

  • Clear price before work starts.
  • Same-day appointments when available.
  • An answered phone 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.
  • We explain what we find and what your options are.
  • We do not push work that you do not need.

Where we work

Our home base is Santa Paula, ZIP codes 93060 and 93061, on the Santa Clara River along State Route 126. We also serve Fillmore, Ojai and Ventura. See the service area page.

What happens when you call

  1. We listen

    You tell us the address, what you see and when it started.

  2. We plan

    We ask about the age of the home and whether the water is shut off.

  3. We book

    We offer a same-day appointment when available and explain what to expect.

  4. You approve

    You get a clear price before work starts, and the work begins after you agree.

If the problem is an emergency, such as a burst pipe or a sewer backup, we say what to do while you wait. That usually means closing the main valve and keeping everyone away from the water.

Why we are call-only

Plumbing problems change quickly, and a form cannot tell us whether the water is still running. A phone call can. We would rather spend two minutes on the line with you than lose a day waiting for an email reply.

It also keeps the site honest. Every button on these pages is a phone link, so there is nothing to fill out and nothing that disappears into an inbox.

Local knowledge that matters to a repair

Santa Paula has a historic downtown on Main Street with older homes, newer areas such as Harvest at Limoneira and a river on its southern edge. Homes, soil and water vary across those areas. We ask where you live and how old the house is, which helps us plan the visit.

For the valley, we dispatch east along State Route 126 to Fillmore, north on State Route 150 to Ojai and west to Ventura. Each has its own town page with details.

Homes and businesses we help

Most of our calls come from homeowners with a drain, a leak or a water heater that has stopped working. We also take calls from renters, landlords, property managers and small businesses. If you are a tenant, we can work with your manager on access and approval, and we keep a clear record of what was repaired.

For shops and restaurants, we help with floor drains, grease buildup, restroom fixtures and water heaters, including after hours. See hydro jetting and sewer line cleaning for the work we do on commercial lines.

What we do not claim

We keep this site to what we can verify, so there is nothing here we cannot back up. We do not quote prices from memory, and we do not make code or permit claims for your address. Ask the City of Santa Paula building department what applies to your project, and ask your water utility about your water.

Call recording notice: calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Our team uses plain language. If a repair is not worth doing, we say so, and if a cheaper fix will hold, we offer it. You decide what happens at your property, and nothing starts until you approve the price.

Safety first

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas company and 911 from outside. If water is near electrical equipment, stay away from it. We can help with the plumbing once it is safe.

Talk to us
